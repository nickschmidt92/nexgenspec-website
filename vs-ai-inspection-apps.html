Two different bets

Most inspection tools are making one of two bets about where the hard part of the job actually is. Neither is wrong — but they lead to very different products.

Bet #1 — the words. Many new AI-first inspection apps assume the bottleneck is writing. So they lead with voice-to-text dictation, AI-generated narratives and summaries, and an all-in-one business suite around it — scheduling, CRM, online booking, and card payments. The report becomes a text document you produce faster. What these apps typically do not include is any way to capture the space itself: no LiDAR, no 3D, no floor plans.

Bet #2 — the capture and the proof. NexGenSpec assumes the durable value is in what the report shows and what it proves. So it leads with LiDAR room scans that can assemble into a whole-home floor plan, an exportable 3D model (USDZ) of each scanned room, annotated photo and video evidence, and a PDF that is fingerprinted and e-signed so it can’t be quietly altered later. You still write your own findings — NexGenSpec makes them spatial and defensible.
